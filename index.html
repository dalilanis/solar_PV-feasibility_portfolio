Executive Summary
Prefeasibility assessment for a 125 kWp rooftop and ground-mounted solar PV installation

This study assesses the technical and financial viability of installing a 125 kWp grid-connected solar PV system at the Cooperative Agricole Drâa-Tafilalet cold storage and food processing facility in Ouarzazate, Morocco. The facility currently sources 60% from diesel generation (€0.35/kWh effective) and 40% from the national grid (€0.12/kWh), yielding a blended cost of €0.26/kWh against 220,000 kWh annual consumption.

The financial model demonstrates exceptional returns: a payback of just 13 months, an NPV of €936,184 over 25 years, and an IRR of 90.95%. The project's LCOE of €0.0236/kWh represents a 91% reduction versus the current blended tariff. Over the project lifetime, the facility realizes a net financial benefit of €1.78 million.

Recommendation: Proceed with Project

The project demonstrates exceptional financial returns driven by Ouarzazate's world-class solar resource (2,486 kWh/m²/year in-plane irradiation) and the facility's elevated blended electricity tariff. Financial Model v3.1 — all 12 automated audit checks passed.
